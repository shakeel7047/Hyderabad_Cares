Common questions

Do I need an account to report?

No. Reporting is open to everyone. An account is only useful if you want to manage several reports from one place.

Is my name or phone number shown publicly?

No. Contact details are for updates only. The public report shows the issue, the area, the photo, and the status — not your identity.

What do the statuses mean?

Reported — submitted and on the map. Community verified — neighbours confirmed it. In progress — the responsible office or team has taken it up. Resolved — fixed, with evidence where possible.

How long does a fix take?

It depends on the category and the office involved. Streetlights and garbage are usually quicker; drainage and road work take longer. The public status is the honest answer — we do not promise dates we cannot control.

Someone already reported my issue. What now?

Confirm their report instead of creating a duplicate. Confirmations are what move a report to “Community verified”.

Can I report on behalf of someone else?

Yes, if the report is accurate and you are not sharing anyone's private information. Describe the problem, not the person.

What should I not post?

No personal data, no number plates, no photos of faces without consent, no political campaigning, and no accusations against named individuals. Reports are about infrastructure, not people.

Is this an emergency service?

No. For a live hazard, fire, or medical emergency, contact local emergency services first. Use this platform for non-urgent civic problems.

Still stuck?
