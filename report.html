What makes a good report

  • An exact landmark, not just the area name.
  • One problem per report.
  • A photo from the road, in daylight if possible.
  • Who it affects — pedestrians, bikers, residents.

What happens next?

  1. Reported — it appears on the map.
  2. Community verified — neighbours confirm it.
  3. In progress — the responsible office has it.
  4. Resolved — with a photo as evidence.

Emergency?

This platform is not for emergencies. For a live hazard, fire, or medical emergency, call the local emergency services first.
