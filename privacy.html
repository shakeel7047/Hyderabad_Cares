Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Template — have it reviewed before launch.

FixMyHyderabad is a community reporting platform for Hyderabad, Sindh. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it. Replace the placeholders and get legal review before publishing.

1. What we collect

  • Report content: category, area, landmark, description, and any photo you upload.
  • Optional contact details: your name, phone number, or email, if you choose to provide them.
  • Technical data: basic server logs such as IP address, browser type, and timestamps, used for security and debugging.

2. What is public

Report text, area, photos, status, and the report ID are public. Your name and contact details are not shown publicly. Do not put personal information in the description or photo.

3. Why we use it

  • To display and track reports.
  • To let nearby residents verify a report.
  • To send status updates if you shared a contact.
  • To keep the platform safe and prevent abuse.

4. Photos and location

Please avoid faces, number plates, and the inside of private homes. If a photo contains personal data, we may crop or remove it. Location is described by area and landmark, not by your device's precise coordinates unless you choose to share them.

5. Cookies and analytics

This demo site sets no cookies and loads no analytics. If you add analytics later, update this section and provide a consent mechanism where required.

6. Sharing

We share report details with the relevant municipal office or volunteer team so the issue can be acted on. We do not sell personal data. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Retention

Public reports are kept as a civic record. Contact details are kept only as long as needed for updates, then deleted on request. Server logs are kept for a short period for security.

8. Your choices

You can ask us to remove your contact details, correct a report, or delete a report you submitted. Email hello@fixmyhyderabad.example with the report ID. Some content may be retained in backups for a limited period.

9. Children

The platform is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child submitted personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change, and significant changes will be announced on the site.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: hello@fixmyhyderabad.example. Replace this with your real contact address.

This is a template, not legal advice. Privacy law depends on where you and your users are, so have a local lawyer review it before you launch.
