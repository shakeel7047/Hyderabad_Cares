About

A city that keeps its own record

FixMyHyderabad is an independent, community-run reporting platform for Hyderabad, Sindh. It exists so that small, everyday problems do not stay invisible.

Why we built this

Most people notice the same problems every day: a broken streetlight, a blocked drain, a footpath that disappears after rain. Individually these feel too small to chase. Together they decide how a neighbourhood feels to live in.

A public list changes the conversation. It shows what is broken, who has confirmed it, and how long it has been waiting.

What we are not

We are not a government department, a contractor, or a news channel. We cannot fix anything ourselves, and we do not promise a deadline we do not control.

What we can do is keep a clear, public record — and make it easier for the right people to act.

How a report moves

1

Submitted

Anyone can report, with or without an account. Each report gets a public ID.

2

Community verified

Two nearby residents confirm the problem is real. Duplicates are merged instead of deleted.

3

Followed up

Verified reports are shared with the relevant office and with local volunteers who can push them.

4

Closed with proof

A report is only marked resolved when there is an after photo or a site confirmation.

Public by default

Every status change is visible. No private tick-box that quietly closes a report.

Non-political

No party flags, no rallies, no blame campaigns. Just the issue and its status.

Privacy first

Contact details stay private. Photos of faces and private property are avoided.
